Academics

Our Curriculum Overview

A Commitment to Excellence

Ijebu Ode Grammar School aims at offering all our young boys a broad and balanced curriculum that provides rewarding and stimulating activities to prepare them for a life of leadership, social distinction, and cultural excellence.

Whether it is our books or hands-on training, we make sure each student gets personal attention to cope up and flourish in every subject for better scores and a brighter future.

Classroom Teaching

Hands-on experience with the latest teaching techniques and technology. We have trained professional teachers who work closely with our students so they are prepared for the real world.

Guided Prep

Guided prep to students who’d like special interest in creative activities and take one up as a career or just a hobby. Our students are trained by professionals who help them develop, learn and polish their skills while at school.

Cognitive Enhancement

We take pride in our very professional staff who are exceptionally skilled in enhancement of our students cognitive abilities.

Clinic

The school has a well equipped clinic to handle cases of students medical needs or emergency with ambulance available for cases that need to be referred to a hospital where the school already has a prearranged agreement with.

Mathematics

Understanding the game of numbers and logic to solve real world problems. Learn mathematics from scholars and university toppers who not make it interesting, but also fun to learn.
