Mission Statement

Ijebu Ode Grammar School is a premier institution dedicated to the excellence and character of young boys. Our mission is to create a conducive atmosphere of sound academic standards, balanced social and psychological formation, and a strong moral foundation that prepares our students for global leadership.

As a school built on tradition and integrity, we ensure that every young boy is formed with the values of discipline and service, fostering a spirit of excellence in all ramifications of life.

Vision Statement

Forming future leaders of high reputation and great value young boys who will positively impact their environment and act as courageous agents of change.

Our Aim

  1. Potential & Excellence: Giving each young boy adequate support and encouragement to reach his full potential and excel in his chosen field.
  2. Resilient Curriculum: Providing a curriculum where students embrace challenges, develop a positive attitude toward life, and foster a lifelong love for learning within the school and the wider society.
  3. Confidence & Leadership: Nurturing confidence and promoting independence, while empowering our young boys to make informed, responsible decisions and choices about their future.
  4. Holistic Development: Providing every student the opportunity to study, learn the value of brotherhood, develop the skills to cope with future challenges, and grow through recreation and prayer for the fuller development of the young boy.
  5. Character & Respect: Cultivating in each student a deep respect for himself, for others, and a spirit of kindness and integrity at all times.
